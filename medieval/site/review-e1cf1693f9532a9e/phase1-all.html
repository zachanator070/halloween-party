Baron Godfrey Aldous de Ravenmoor

Lord of Ravenmoor Keep, your gracious host
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Zach Johnson. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the proud, slightly anxious master of Ravenmoor, a modest barony you describe as 'practically a kingdom'. Tonight's feast is your grandest ever. A foreign queen has honoured your hall with a visit and you mean to impress her, charm the King, and sign a trade charter. You know almost nothing about the queen except that she has come from very far away with an escort and a great many trunks, and you are desperate to learn more.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You COUNT everything aloud as you go ('Seven candles! Splendid!', 'Four guests by the fire, a fine number!') and you introduce every guest with an absurd, grand title you invent on the spot ('The Duke of Sunsets', 'Her Grace the Countess of Pudding'). You are certain your cellar is the finest in the shire.

How you treat others

  • Greet everyone as the most honoured guest you have ever had, then forget their name and invent a new title.
  • Flatter the King and Queen constantly and fuss that they have the best chairs.
  • Hover around the foreign queen and beg her for stories of her homeland, then nod as if you already knew.
  • Whenever money is mentioned, change the subject to your cellar.

Your relationships

  • Lord Reginald Thornbury: Your oldest friend from boyhood, and also, awkwardly, a debtor: you and Master Crowe lent him a great deal.
  • Master Bartholomew Crowe: Your business partner in lending and trade. You suspect he cheats you, but you need him.
  • King Oswin: Your liege. His approval is your favourite food.

Your flaw

Vanity. You cannot bear to be corrected, and you exaggerate everything you know.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT MASTER CROWE: his famous 'Eastern silk' is dyed English wool. You have known for months and have said nothing because you profit too. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Props to bring

  • A big ring of keys (any keys!)
  • A goblet or fancy mug to wave around
  • A rolled paper 'guest list' you consult and ignore
  • A cape: a bedsheet or bathrobe with a pinned-on brooch

Costume tip: A velvet-looking robe or bathrobe, a belt, fur trim from a scarf, a slightly crooked small crown or cap.

Your gift

The host's household provides a flask of 'the finest spiced mead in the shire' (it is mostly cider) for the Exalted Lady, and sets it on the gift table for you. You taste it first at the toast, by the custom of the house.

Conversation starters

  • 'Have you seen my cellar? Four hundred casks!' (it is not four hundred.)
  • 'Did you count the candles? Eleven!'
  • 'Tell me, what do you know of our esteemed guest?'
Keep this page secret. More will be revealed at the feast.

Queen Zhao Suyin of Cathay

Sovereign of Cathay, the Honoured Guest from the Far East
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Alyssum Johnson. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the Queen of Cathay, a great kingdom at the far eastern edge of the world. After two years on the long road you have reached the western lands to see them for yourself and find trading partners, because your court has grown bored of its own tea. You are calm, observant, wise, and quietly amused by European customs: eating with knives, bathing in rivers, and shouting at feasts.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

After each strange European custom, raise ONE eyebrow, say 'In Cathay...' and describe something that is the exact opposite. Whenever someone is upset, nervous, or arguing, you stop everything and ceremonially pour them tea, with a small bow. You never hurry.

How you treat others

  • Speak slowly and formally. Thank everyone for the smallest courtesy.
  • Be politely curious about the King (you outrank him in manners and let him know with a tiny smile).
  • Amused but wary of merchants who haggle. Let Master Crowe talk himself into trouble.
  • Refuse to discuss business until everyone has been served tea.

Your relationships

  • Master Bartholomew Crowe: A persistent merchant who keeps offering you 'authentic Cathay silk'. You find it hilarious and slightly insulting.
  • Baron Godfrey: Your kind but exhausting host. He counts things a great deal.
  • Queen Margarethe: You admire her command of a room and secretly want to win the 'who is more regal' contest.
  • Wen Liang: Your interpreter and companion, if he is in attendance. A trusted old friend.

Your flaw

Rigid about protocol. You will not be hurried, interrupted, or served out of order, and you hold a grudge for a bad pour of tea.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT QUEEN MARGARETHE: the large pearl necklace she wears is glass. A queen of Cathay knows real pearls by their glow. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Props to bring

  • A teapot or kettle and a few small cups (or mugs)
  • A folding fan or paper fan
  • A silk robe or long scarf (a kimono or bathrobe works)
  • Chopsticks as hairpins; a pen or two

Costume tip: A long, flowing robe in red or gold, hair up and held with chopsticks, calm expression, a hint of dramatic eye makeup.

Conversation starters

  • 'In Cathay, a host drinks first. Tell me, is this custom also yours?'
  • 'May I pour you some tea? You seem agitated.'

Who you are

You are the cheerful, round, and not-especially-bright King of Aldermere, who loves feasting, hunting, and being told he is wise. You rule mostly by agreeing with whoever spoke last. You have come to Ravenmoor mostly for the food, and partly because Queen Margarethe made you.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You refer to yourself as 'We' (and sometimes forget and say 'me'). You fall asleep in the middle of sentences and wake up suddenly shouting a food ('...and thus the treaty—PIE!'). You carry a snack, always, and are always hungry.

How you treat others

  • Be delighted by everyone, but especially anyone who offers you food.
  • Defer to Queen Margarethe on everything, loudly.
  • Treat the Jester as your best friend; ask for a joke every few minutes.
  • Doze off whenever anyone speaks about trade, taxes, or politics for more than a sentence.

Your relationships

  • Tobias Thistlewick, the Jester: Your closest friend. He tells you the truth and you pretend it is a joke.

Your flaw

Gluttony and gullibility. You will believe anything, if it is said with confidence, and you can be bribed with sweets.

Who you are

You are the sharp, elegant, deeply bossy Queen of Aldermere. You run the kingdom, the household, the King's diet, and everyone's posture. You have come to Ravenmoor chiefly to ensure the new foreign queen does not outshine you.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You SNAP your fan shut to silence a room and give commands in a clipped monotone ('Sit. Eat. Smile. Not like that.'). You correct everyone's manners, posture, and grammar, even strangers'. You sniff at things and say 'Hm.'

How you treat others

  • Command, correct, and instruct. Everyone should sit up straighter.

  • Hover protectively over the King and cut his dessert for him.

Your flaw

Control. You cannot stand being unprepared and you must have the last word.

It is All Hallows' Eve and you are NOT comfortable.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

Every story starts 'In my forty victories...' and the number changes each time ('thirty-eight', 'fifty-one'). You make tiny involuntary flinches at spooky noises (creaks, bells, giggles) and then say 'Hah! A knight is never startled!' very loudly.

How you treat others

  • Puff up your chest around other men of rank; challenge the Jester to a verbal duel and lose.
  • Bow low to the Queens and compliment their courage.
  • Treat your squire Wat with gruff affection and constantly ask him to hold something.
  • Avoid anything spooky; change the subject whenever ghosts, bats, or the dark are mentioned.

Your relationships

  • Squire Wat Fenwick: Your squire, your shadow, your biggest fan. You teach him everything (badly).
  • Queen Margarethe: You are her champion. You would die for her and she knows it and bosses you.
  • Baron Godfrey: He (and a merchant partner) lent you 50 marks, which you have not repaid. You avoid him by loudly telling stories.

Your flaw

A secret coward who is dying to be seen as a hero. You also owe money you pretend you don't.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT MAGISTER MORDECAI: his 'magical lightning' is flash powder. You saw him mixing it in the stable behind the royal tent last spring. Trade this for information.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Props to bring

  • A cardboard, foam, or wooden sword with a scabbard

  • A colander, pot, or helmet-shaped hat

Costume tip: A tunic with a tabard over a long-sleeved shirt, a belt with a sword, boots, an enormous mustache (drawn or glued).

You gasp at 'omens' in ordinary things ('The salt spilled to the LEFT!' 'Three candles! An omen!') and make vague, ominous predictions that could mean anything. You pull coins from people's ears and sleeves. You speak in a hushed, theatrical whisper and wave your sleeves.

How you treat others

  • Tell the King his future is bright, which he loves.
  • Be wary of the Jester; he is the one person who sees through you.

Your relationships

  • King Oswin: Your patron. He believes everything you say, and you take care to say very little.

Your flaw

Total fraud, terrified of being exposed. You panic when asked to perform real magic or to explain how your tricks work.

You cannot resist mocking anyone and everyone. It has made you enemies. You also hate being quiet.

Merchant of the Wool Guild, Crowe & Company
