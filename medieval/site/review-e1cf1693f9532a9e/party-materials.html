How to print: Letter paper, landscape, default margins, scale 100%, Background graphics ON. Phase 2 cards print two to a sheet, evidence cards four to a sheet, bonus clue cards, final ballots and award certificates four to a sheet. Cut along the card borders. The invitation, Phase 1 pages and Phase 2 cards are digital (the Phase 2 card unlocks on each player's page with the passphrase the host reveals); these printed Phase 2 cards are an optional fallback. Evidence cards and bonus clue cards are physical. Keep them sealed until the host's signal.

Phase 2
Your Character

Your afternoon

From half past five until the seven o'clock bell you were in the kitchens, tasting soups with the cook. You counted fourteen soups (it was fifteen; you will say fourteen). You left only once, to shout at a kitchen boy.

What else you know

At supper the night before, you and Master Crowe argued about announcing your debtors' names at the toast: Crowe insisted that 'shame pays faster than silver'. You called it ungentlemanly. He laughed. Today, Lord Thornbury spent the whole evening turning the spit beside you, complaining that it was beneath him.

Your new secret to trade: ABOUT LORD THORNBURY: you saw him slip into the kitchen at half past five to pilfer comfits (he has stolen sweets from your kitchen since he was a boy). The cook caught him and put him to work at the spit until the seven o'clock bell. He did not leave once.
Your conviction: You are firmly convinced that Tobias the Jester is the murderer. He mocked you at the last feast and you threatened to banish him, and a jester who hates a host would sooner poison the party than leave it. You do not know what clears him.

Your evidence

You were handed a piece of evidence: The Cook's Kitchen Chit. You may show it, describe it, or trade it for secrets or for other evidence. Whoever holds it can read it.

Your quest

Seek: Master Crowe's Day Ledger. Your partner kept his ledger of sales and loans. Find it (Crowe's ghost has it) and turn it in. Ask nicely: ghosts cannot carry things. (When you do, the host will give you your Bonus Clues.)

Your goal

Work out WHO killed Master Crowe, HOW, and WHY. At the end of the party, fill in your Final Ballot (your accusation, plus your votes for Best Costume and Best Actor) and hand it to the host. Coins you hold count toward Richest Purse; trade them freely for information. Remember: you may even be wrong about everyone, including yourself.

The Ghost of Ravenmoor

Master Bartholomew Crowe, now deceased
Player: Shelby Gorriceta
Phase 2
Ghost

Your new role

  • You are DEAD. You are now the Ghost of Master Crowe. Put on a sheet or a pale veil, if you have one, and wander the Hall silently and mournfully. Do not speak, except to answer questions.
  • Any living guest may ask you yes-or-no questions, but you may answer only THREE in total across the whole evening. Answer by nodding or shaking your head, then name a price for the answer in a hollow ghostly whisper ('Yes. Dear at twice the price.').
  • Answer truthfully using the list below. If asked something not on the list, shake your head sadly (you do not know).
  • You still carry your Phase 1 secret, which you may share by nodding only if asked about it directly.

Your truthful answers (use only if asked)

Was the poison in your tin of candied ginger? Yes.
Was it wolfsbane (monkshood)? Yes.
Was the tin shut when you set it on the gift table at a quarter past five? Yes.
Did you see who tampered with it? No. It came as a surprise to you.
Were you taking tea with Queen Suyin from half past five until seven? Yes. You never left her side.
Was the Hall barred to guests from half past five until the bell at seven? Yes.
Did you sell three pouches of wolfsbane? Yes. They are all in your ledger.
Did you plan to announce debts at the toast? Yes. Two of them.

Your evidence

You hold Master Crowe's Day Ledger. Living guests may read it or trade for it.

Your quest

Seek: The Interpreter's Tea Log. You wish to see how many offers you made at tea (you counted a great many). Find Wen's log and turn it in. Ask nicely: ghosts cannot carry things. (The host will give you Bonus Clues.)

Gold and awards

You keep the gold you had and may still earn coins for your quest. Coins count toward Richest Purse, and you may vote for Best Costume and Best Actor.

You do not know who killed you. Enjoy haunting.

You win by watching them all fumble.

Coins you hold count toward Richest Purse; trade them freely for information. Remember: you may even be wrong about everyone, including yourself.

Phase 2 card. Speak in character, as before. Trade freely.
