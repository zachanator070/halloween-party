Tobias "Tumble" Thistlewick

Court Jester to King Oswin
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Kayla Beckstead. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the King's beloved court jester: wise as a judge, silly as a goose. You tell the truth to everyone, always disguised as a joke, so you may say what no one else can. You are rarely taken seriously, which suits you perfectly.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You speak mostly in RHYME (even badly: 'oh the mead, I do need!'), your bells jingle with every move, and you deliver insults as compliments ('Sir, your mind is as sharp as a bowl of pudding!'). You cartwheel when surprised and bow at the end of every performance.

How you treat others

  • Tease everyone gently, especially the powerful. Poke fun at the knight, the magician, and the Baron.
  • Be fiercely loyal to King Oswin; keep him laughing.
  • Speak a hard truth, then follow it with 'Just a jest!'
  • Be warm and silly with the Squire and the Princess.

Your relationships

  • King Oswin: Your friend and your patron. He is a child, and you love him.
  • Magister Mordecai: Your rival. You can duplicate any of his tricks but he cannot do yours.
  • Baron Godfrey: He found your mockery rude and threatened to banish you from his halls.

Your flaw

You cannot resist mocking anyone and everyone. It has made you enemies. You also hate being quiet.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT QUEEN MARGARETHE: she sings in the bath, loudly and badly, and has begged you never to tell. You were walking past the bath-house and heard 'Greensleeves' butchered. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Costume and prop ideas

These are only suggestions. Dress however you think best suits your character and your budget: homemade, borrowed, improvised or imagined all count, and a hat and a good attitude are plenty.

  • A jingling hat or bells (tie small bells to your sleeves)
  • A stick topped with a sock puppet or stuffed toy
  • Three balls or socks to (almost) juggle
  • Mismatched socks, tights, or colourful clothing

Costume idea: Bright, mismatched colours, a floppy two-pointed hat with bells, striped tights, a big painted smile.

Conversation starters

  • 'Good sir, you are as wise as a bell, and just as hollow!'
  • 'A knight, a mage, and a king walk into a feast... and only one is sober!'
  • 'Hear the tale of Wat the Squire, who dropped a lance, and then his hire!'
