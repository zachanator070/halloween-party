Wen Liang

Interpreter and Escort to the Queen of Cathay
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Emily Barber. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are Queen Suyin's loyal interpreter and companion, who has travelled with her for two years. You speak the western tongue fluently, with very dry humour. You translate for the Queen, but you add your own opinion. You are the only person who knows what she really thinks.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You TRANSLATE the Queen's flowery pronouncements into blunt, deadpan one-liners ('She says she is honoured to dwell in your hospitality.' 'Translation: the soup is fine.'). You speak without expression, no matter what is happening. You occasionally 'misinterpret' on purpose, to mischief.

How you treat others

  • Stand just behind the Queen and translate for her. Be dry and unflappable.
  • Give honest, brutal translations of what Europeans say, too ('He says he is delighted. He is not.').
  • Be wary of anyone who tries to sell things to the Queen.
  • Be quietly polite and very, very funny.

Your relationships

  • Queen Suyin: Your queen and friend. You know her moods by her fan.
  • Master Crowe: He tries to sell you things between sentences. You enjoy ruining his deals.
  • Magister Mordecai: He tried to buy 'something exciting' from the Queen's baggage. You said no.

Your flaw

You mistranslate on purpose when bored. It has already caused one treaty near-disaster.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT QUEEN SUYIN: she adores English custard tarts and ate eleven on the road. She has begged you never to tell. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it only in exchange for information. You may bluff, but you may not show anyone this page.

Props to bring

  • A scroll or rolled paper
  • A small notebook and a quill or pen
  • A sash or silk belt
  • A teacup, always on a saucer

Costume tip: A simple dark silk robe with a sash, hair tied back, completely neutral expression.

Gift to bring for the Baron: A scroll with the Baron's name written in beautiful Cathay characters (it says 'Honoured Host' - maybe).

Conversation starters

  • 'She says it is a pleasure. It is... a pleasure.'
  • 'I will translate that. She says no.'
  • 'Do not mention custard tarts. I beg you.'
Keep this page secret. More will be revealed at the feast.
