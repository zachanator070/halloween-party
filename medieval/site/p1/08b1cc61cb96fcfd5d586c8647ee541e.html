Magister Mordecai Quillfeather

Court Magician to King Oswin
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Brad Stephenson. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the King's court magician and the most mysterious man in the kingdom. You are also a fraud. Your 'magic' is stagecraft, flash powder, card tricks, and cheap almanacs. You must never be caught, because the King believes in you, and the King's belief is your whole income.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You gasp at 'omens' in ordinary things ('The salt spilled to the LEFT!' 'Three candles! An omen!') and make vague, ominous predictions that could mean anything. You pull coins from people's ears and sleeves. You speak in a hushed, theatrical whisper and wave your sleeves.

How you treat others

  • Be mysterious and dramatic with everyone; read palms and tea leaves.
  • Tell the King his future is bright, which he loves.
  • Be wary of the Jester; he is the one person who sees through you.
  • Avoid Queen Margarethe's gaze; she hates you.

Your relationships

  • King Oswin: Your patron. He believes everything you say, and you take care to say very little.
  • Tobias Thistlewick, the Jester: Your rival. He mocks your tricks and you hate it.
  • Queen Margarethe: She distrusts you. You are very polite and sweaty near her.

Your flaw

Total fraud, terrified of being exposed. You panic when asked to perform real magic or to explain how your tricks work.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT TOBIAS THE JESTER: he writes the rude ballads about the court that are sung in every tavern. His rhymes give him away: he always rhymes 'queen' with 'obscene'. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Props to bring

  • A pointy hat or hood and a long robe (a bathrobe!)
  • A deck of cards or some coins for tricks
  • A glass bowl or ball as a 'crystal ball'
  • A wand: a chopstick, stick, or wooden spoon

Costume tip: A starry dark robe, a pointed hat, rings, a long fake beard if you can. Practice one coin trick.

Conversation starters

  • 'Hush! Do you not hear it? The omens are... stirring.'
  • 'Allow me to read your palm. Ah. I see... a visitor. Or a loss.'
  • 'The coin! It was behind your ear all along.'
Keep this page secret. More will be revealed at the feast.
