King Oswin Edmund Plantagrey IV

By the Grace of Heaven, King of Aldermere
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Craig Brown. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the cheerful, round, and not-especially-bright King of Aldermere, who loves feasting, hunting, and being told he is wise. You rule mostly by agreeing with whoever spoke last. You have come to Ravenmoor mostly for the food, and partly because Queen Margarethe made you.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You refer to yourself as 'We' (and sometimes forget and say 'me'). You fall asleep in the middle of sentences and wake up suddenly shouting a food ('...and thus the treaty—PIE!'). You carry a snack, always, and are always hungry.

How you treat others

  • Be delighted by everyone, but especially anyone who offers you food.
  • Defer to Queen Margarethe on everything, loudly.
  • Treat the Jester as your best friend; ask for a joke every few minutes.
  • Doze off whenever anyone speaks about trade, taxes, or politics for more than a sentence.

Your relationships

  • Queen Margarethe: Your wife, the real ruler of Aldermere. You are terrified and adoring.
  • Tobias Thistlewick, the Jester: Your closest friend. He tells you the truth and you pretend it is a joke.
  • Magister Mordecai: Your court magician. You believe every word he says.

Your flaw

Gluttony and gullibility. You will believe anything, if it is said with confidence, and you can be bribed with sweets.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT LORD THORNBURY: at the last royal feast he stole your marzipan swan, tucked it into his coat, and ate it behind a curtain. You saw. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it only in exchange for information. You may bluff, but you may not show anyone this page.

Props to bring

  • A paper or cardboard crown
  • A scepter (a wooden spoon, rolling pin, or long candle)
  • A cape or blanket draped over your shoulders
  • A drumstick or snack in your pocket at all times

Costume tip: A red robe or tablecloth cape, golden belt, crown slightly askew, a napkin tucked in your collar.

Gift to bring for the Baron: A signed (by someone else) royal decree granting the Baron 'the right to be honoured by the King', plus a jar of honey.

Conversation starters

  • 'We are very hungry. Is there... pie?'
  • 'Where is our jester? We require a joke.'
  • 'Is it time for the feast? What time is it? Is it feast time?'
Keep this page secret. More will be revealed at the feast.
