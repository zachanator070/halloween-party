Lucien Fairweather

Troubadour, Minstrel of the Roads
Phase 1
Your Character

Who you are

You are a wandering troubadour who has come to Ravenmoor hoping to play for the royal court, and be paid. You are charming, vain, and entirely certain that you are the greatest songwriter in the realm. You are broke and always on the lookout for a rhyme.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You burst into improvised SONG whenever there is tension, silence, or a pause ('Oh, the soup is hot, and the guests are not...'), and you rhyme at the end of every second sentence. You strike a pose after each verse and wait for applause.

How you treat others

  • Serenade people (especially the Princess), whether they asked or not.
  • Compete with the Jester for attention.
  • Be delighted by any drama; it's material.
  • Angle for payment from the Baron or the King.

Your relationships

  • Princess Isolde: Your muse. You serenade her at every opportunity.
  • Tobias Thistlewick, the Jester: Your rival for the royal ear.
  • Baron Godfrey: He has not yet paid your fee from last Easter.

Your flaw

You steal other minstrels' tunes and swear they're yours. You also never pay back anything you borrow.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT TOBIAS THE JESTER: he cannot actually juggle. His juggling balls are stuffed socks, and he has dropped them in every performance for years. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it only in exchange for information. You may bluff, but you may not show anyone this page.

Props to bring

  • A lute, ukulele, guitar, or tambourine
  • A feathered cap or hat
  • A small songbook or lyric sheet
  • A rose to present (to anyone)

Costume tip: A colourful tunic, a cape, a feathered hat, boots, a dramatic scarf.

Gift to bring for the Baron: A song about the Baron, performed on the spot, with an invoice.

Conversation starters

  • 'I have composed a ballad about this very moment!'
  • 'Shall I sing? I shall sing.'
  • 'Who here has a rhyme for "Baron"? Only "barren"...'
