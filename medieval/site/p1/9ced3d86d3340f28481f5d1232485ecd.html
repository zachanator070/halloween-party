Wat Piers Fenwick

Squire to Sir Cedric the Valiant
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Breyton Barber. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are a sweet, overeager young squire, clumsy and endlessly loyal to Sir Cedric. You can write a clear hand, which makes you useful to everyone who needs a letter copied. You carry much more than you should and you drop at least one thing per conversation.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You REPEAT the last few words of anything Sir Cedric says, in awe ('Forty victories!' 'Forty!'), then add 'Indeed!' You cannot lie without going bright red and stammering. You are always carrying something and apologising.

How you treat others

  • Admire Sir Cedric loudly, at all times.
  • Be shy around the Princess (if she is present); blush and drop something.
  • Be helpful to everyone: offer to carry, fetch, or copy anything.
  • If asked a direct question, answer too honestly, then regret it.

Your relationships

  • Sir Cedric: Your knight, your hero, your boss. You love him, even though you know his stories are fibs.
  • Tobias Thistlewick, the Jester: Your friend. He teaches you rhymes and tricks.
  • Princess Isolde: If she attends: your secret crush. You write poems you never show.

Your flaw

You cannot lie. Any direct question gets a stammering, over-honest answer, and you cannot keep a secret for long.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT SIR CEDRIC: he is terrified of ghosts, geese, and the dark, and he sleeps with a candle burning. You hold his gauntlets while he shivers. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Costume and prop ideas

These are only suggestions. Dress however you think best suits your character and your budget: homemade, borrowed, improvised or imagined all count, and a hat and a good attitude are plenty.

  • A tray piled with random objects (cups, a sock, a shoe)
  • A polishing cloth over your shoulder
  • A quill pen and a small roll of paper
  • A tote bag or satchel marked 'GEAR'

Costume idea: A plain tunic, belt, boots, hair slightly messy, a smudge of ink on your cheek.

Conversation starters

  • 'Sir Cedric says he has done forty victories!' 'Forty!'
  • 'May I carry that for you? Oh dear, I dropped something.'
  • 'I have a scroll of poems if... never mind, it is nothing.'
Keep this page secret. More will be revealed at the feast.
