Princess Isolde Rosalind Plantagrey

Princess of Aldermere, daughter of King Oswin
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Paige Stephenson. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the dreamy, romantic, dramatic young Princess of Aldermere. You believe the world is a ballad and you are its heroine. You have a lot of feelings, a lot of sighs, and a very clean handkerchief.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You SIGH loudly at nothing ('Ohhh...'), clutch your heart at mention of love, poetry, or the moon, and FAINT dramatically (onto the pillow or sofa) at anything alarming. You cry 'It's just like the ballads!' about everything.

How you treat others

  • Be romantic about every situation and gently swoon.
  • Be excessively sweet to the squire and the minstrel. Gaze longingly.
  • Be embarrassed by your mother's bossiness; hide behind your fan.
  • If anyone mentions 'duty', run away or faint.

Your relationships

  • Queen Margarethe and King Oswin: Your parents. Your mother organises your life and your father forgets it.
  • Squire Wat Fenwick: The sweet squire who dropped a tray at court. You think of him often. A little too often.
  • Lucien the Troubadour: He writes songs about you. They are not good, but you swoon anyway.

Your flaw

Naive and impulsive. You believe any sad story and run from any responsibility.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT SQUIRE WAT: he carries a poem folded in his tunic that begins 'O Princess of the starry hair'. You peeked. You did not tell him you peeked. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Costume and prop ideas

These are only suggestions. Dress however you think best suits your character and your budget: homemade, borrowed, improvised or imagined all count, and a hat and a good attitude are plenty.

  • A flower crown or a few real/paper flowers
  • A 'love letter' on scented paper
  • A pillow or cushion for fainting safely
  • A hand mirror or ribbon

Costume idea: A pastel gown or nightgown with a long scarf as a veil, flowers in your hair, flushed cheeks.

Conversation starters

  • 'Ohhh, is it not exactly like a ballad?' *sigh*
  • 'Have you ever loved someone whose name you couldn't say?'
  • 'I feel faint... Oh, there is a lovely cushion!'
Keep this page secret. More will be revealed at the feast.
