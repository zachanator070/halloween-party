Queen Margarethe Eleanor Plantagrey

Queen of Aldermere, Consort of King Oswin
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Emily Brown. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the sharp, elegant, deeply bossy Queen of Aldermere. You run the kingdom, the household, the King's diet, and everyone's posture. You have come to Ravenmoor chiefly to ensure the new foreign queen does not outshine you.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You SNAP your fan shut to silence a room and give commands in a clipped monotone ('Sit. Eat. Smile. Not like that.'). You correct everyone's manners, posture, and grammar, even strangers'. You sniff at things and say 'Hm.'

How you treat others

  • Command, correct, and instruct. Everyone should sit up straighter.
  • Be icily polite to the foreign queen, secretly rivalrous: you want the better chair.
  • Hover protectively over the King and cut his dessert for him.
  • Be suspicious of the Magician; roll your eyes when he predicts anything.

Your relationships

  • King Oswin: Your husband. You love him dearly and treat him like a child.
  • Lady Rosamund Thornbury: Your cousin, fallen on hard times. You pity her and pretend not to.
  • Magister Mordecai: The court magician you cannot stand. He mutters about poisons and potions.

Your flaw

Control. You cannot stand being unprepared and you must have the last word.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT MAGISTER MORDECAI: his 'prophecies' are copied from a cheap street almanac he hides in his sleeve. You found it. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Props to bring

  • A hand fan (to snap)
  • A small bell or spoon to tap on a glass
  • A tiara or shiny headband
  • White gloves; a lace handkerchief

Costume tip: A long gown or dress, a shawl, pearls, a tall stiff posture, an expression that says 'I am disappointed'.

Conversation starters

  • 'Sit up straight. You look like a sack of turnips.'
  • 'Is that how they hold a goblet in your country? Hm.'
  • 'My husband has no idea where he is. Please be kind.'
