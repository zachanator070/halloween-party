Godwin Alfred Hobsworth

Steward of Ravenmoor Keep
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for John Gorriceta. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the Baron's long-suffering steward, responsible for every door, key, cask, and candle in Ravenmoor. You love rules above all things. Tonight you are in a state of constant, quiet panic, because a royal feast means a thousand things that can go wrong.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You ANNOUNCE rules nobody asked for ('Rule the Seventeenth: no sitting on the stairs!', 'Rule the Forty-First: no whistling before the soup!') and invent a new rule each time someone does something amusing. Your keys jingle constantly, and you sneak little sips from any goblet that is unattended.

How you treat others

  • Fuss over everyone and everything; announce rules and fix chairs.
  • Be loyal to the Baron even when he is ridiculous.
  • Be suspicious of the Jester, who has been known to steal from the kitchen.
  • Be deferential to royalty but secretly more important than any of them.

Your relationships

  • Baron Godfrey: Your master. You adore and fear his enthusiasm.
  • Tobias Thistlewick, the Jester: Your nemesis. He has pinched honey pots and mocked your keys.
  • Brother Ambrose: Your drinking buddy, over a few secret sips in the cellar.

Your flaw

You secretly sneak sips of the Baron's best wine and are hoping nobody counts the barrels.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT BROTHER AMBROSE: he keeps a tiny flask of mead inside his Bible, hollowed out for the purpose. He refills it from the cellar. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it only in exchange for information. You may bluff, but you may not show anyone this page.

Props to bring

  • A very large ring of keys
  • A staff or long broom handle
  • A clipboard or scroll of 'Protocol'
  • A small bell to ring for dinner

Costume tip: A dark livery or apron, a chain of office, a very serious expression, spectacles on the end of the nose.

Gift to bring for the Baron: A hand-lettered 'Rules of the House' scroll, numbered up to 112.

Conversation starters

  • 'Rule the Twenty-Third: do not touch the candelabra.'
  • 'Have you counted the guests? I make it eleven. Or sixteen. Dear me.'
  • 'Is that goblet yours? Whose goblet is that?'
