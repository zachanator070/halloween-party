Queen Zhao Suyin of Cathay

Sovereign of Cathay, the Honoured Guest from the Far East
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Alyssum Johnson. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are the Queen of Cathay, a great kingdom at the far eastern edge of the world. After two years on the long road you have reached the western lands to see them for yourself and find trading partners, because your court has grown bored of its own tea. You are calm, observant, wise, and quietly amused by European customs: eating with knives, bathing in rivers, and shouting at feasts.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

After each strange European custom, raise ONE eyebrow, say 'In Cathay...' and describe something that is the exact opposite. Whenever someone is upset, nervous, or arguing, you stop everything and ceremonially pour them tea, with a small bow. You never hurry.

How you treat others

  • Speak slowly and formally. Thank everyone for the smallest courtesy.
  • Be politely curious about the King (you outrank him in manners and let him know with a tiny smile).
  • Amused but wary of merchants who haggle. Let Master Crowe talk himself into trouble.
  • Refuse to discuss business until everyone has been served tea.

Your relationships

  • Master Bartholomew Crowe: A persistent merchant who keeps offering you 'authentic Cathay silk'. You find it hilarious and slightly insulting.
  • Baron Godfrey: Your kind but exhausting host. He counts things a great deal.
  • Queen Margarethe: You admire her command of a room and secretly want to win the 'who is more regal' contest.
  • Wen Liang: Your interpreter and companion, if he is in attendance. A trusted old friend.

Your flaw

Rigid about protocol. You will not be hurried, interrupted, or served out of order, and you hold a grudge for a bad pour of tea.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT QUEEN MARGARETHE: the large pearl necklace she wears is glass. A queen of Cathay knows real pearls by their glow. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Props to bring

  • A teapot or kettle and a few small cups (or mugs)
  • A folding fan or paper fan
  • A silk robe or long scarf (a kimono or bathrobe works)
  • Chopsticks as hairpins; a pen or two

Costume tip: A long, flowing robe in red or gold, hair up and held with chopsticks, calm expression, a hint of dramatic eye makeup.

Gift to bring for the Baron: Small boxes of fragrant tea for everyone, and a tiny silk fan for the Baron.

Conversation starters

  • 'In Cathay, a host drinks first. Tell me, is this custom also yours?'
  • 'May I pour you some tea? You seem agitated.'
