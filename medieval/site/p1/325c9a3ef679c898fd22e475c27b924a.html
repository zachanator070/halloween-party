Master Bartholomew Crowe

Merchant of the Wool Guild, Crowe & Company
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Shelby Gorriceta. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are a shrewd, chatty, relentless merchant who has never met a thing that couldn't be sold at a profit. You have come to Ravenmoor for one reason: to win the foreign queen's trade, and thereby a monopoly. You sell everything: wool, spices, oil, 'authentic' silks, and the occasional hunter's bait.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You APPRAISE everything and everyone aloud ('That hat? Two shillings! The cloak? Eight!') and you name a price for every object, favour, and secret, even in casual chat. You tally on your fingers and hum when you are doing sums. You haggle even over greetings.

How you treat others

  • Pitch everything to everyone. Everyone is a customer. Price every gift you see.
  • Be fawning to the foreign Queen and the King. Be unctuous. Offer discounts.
  • Avoid Lord Thornbury politely; he owes you money.
  • Give everyone's gift a price tag, loudly.

Your relationships

  • Baron Godfrey: Your partner in loans and trade. You think he is a fool but a profitable one.
  • The foreign Queen: The prize. You will sell her anything. (You will learn who she is when she arrives.)
  • King Oswin: Your biggest debtor. His circlet is in your strongbox.

Your flaw

Greed. You also sell fakes and have a habit of mislabelling goods, 'for efficiency'.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT KING OSWIN: his gold circlet is in pawn to you for a loan that paid for hunting dogs. The King does not wear it because he does not have it. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Props to bring

  • A small tin of candied ginger (or any sweets) to open at the toast
  • An abacus, calculator, or notebook of figures
  • A coin pouch full of tokens or chocolate coins
  • A measuring tape or ruler

Costume tip: A tidy tunic in good brown wool, a cap, a chain of office, a belt with a purse, ink-stained fingers.

Your gift

For the Exalted Lady: a pewter tin of your finest candied ginger, with a small paper price tag. By the custom of the house you taste the first piece yourself at the toast, then you sell her the rest. Set it on the gift table when you arrive; it is presented at the toast.

Conversation starters

  • 'That cloak? Eight shillings. Tell me, what would you pay for a better one?'
  • 'For you, friend, a special price. Just for you.'
  • 'Everything has a price. What is yours?'
