Lady Rosamund Philippa Thornbury

Lady of Thornbury Manor, cousin to the Queen
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Rachel Chandler. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are Lord Thornbury's formidable, impeccably polite wife. Your family fell on hard times a long while ago, and you have not stopped smiling since. You never mention money, you never complain, and you pity everyone who has more of it than you.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You are SYRUPY-sweet and devastatingly polite: 'Oh, how BRAVE of you to wear that!', 'Dear me, what a dish... what is it?' Every compliment contains a small knife. You pat everyone's arm gently while delivering the insult.

How you treat others

  • Be warm, genteel, and kind, then deliver a polite, devastating comment.
  • Defer to your husband in public, and run the show in private.
  • Be loyal to your cousin Queen Margarethe, who fusses over you.
  • Pity merchants. Speak to Master Crowe as if he is a charming puppy.

Your relationships

  • Lord Reginald Thornbury: Your husband. Kind, useless, adored. You manage everything.
  • Queen Margarethe: Your cousin. You love her and resent how kindly she pities you.
  • Brother Ambrose: If he attends: your confessor, who keeps your secrets.

Your flaw

Pride in your family name. You would rather starve in silk than accept charity, and you never admit anything is wrong.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT YOUR HUSBAND: Reginald pockets sweets everywhere he goes, comfits, marzipan, bits of cheese. You find melted comfits in all his coats and have given up. He thinks you don't know. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it only in exchange for information. You may bluff, but you may not show anyone this page.

Props to bring

  • Lace gloves or a lace-trimmed scarf
  • A hand fan
  • Obviously-fake jewels: big bead necklace, clip-on earrings
  • A lorgnette (a pair of glasses on a stick) or a monocle

Costume tip: An old-fashioned, slightly threadbare gown, too many mismatched jewels, perfect posture, flawless smile.

Gift to bring for the Baron: A hand-stitched lavender sachet (the lavender is mostly dried grass) and a thoughtful note.

Conversation starters

  • 'Oh, how BRAVE of you to wear that. Truly.'
  • 'Reginald, dear, put the cheese back.'
  • 'Do you know, my cousin the Queen mentions you often. Mostly as a warning.'
