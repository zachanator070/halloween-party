Lord Reginald Cuthbert Thornbury

Lord of Thornbury Manor (somewhat in the red)
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Ryan Chandler. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are a charming, vague, and thoroughly bankrupt nobleman. Your manor leaks, your servants have left, and your coat is older than most guests. You are tremendously proud and pretend nothing is wrong. You never speak of money, because it is 'so tedious'.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

You say 'Money is so TEDIOUS' whenever the subject comes up, and you discreetly POCKET snacks and small trinkets throughout the evening (bread, cheese, comfits, sugared nuts, an occasional spoon). You are the sort of person who 'forgets' his purse.

How you treat others

  • Act lavishly rich. Wave away bills, dismiss prices, and say 'a trifle'.
  • Be effortlessly charming to everyone, especially people you owe money.
  • Be anxious whenever the Baron or Master Crowe is close; find a reason to slip away.
  • Be fiercely devoted to your wife, and defer to her on every serious decision.

Your relationships

  • Baron Godfrey: Your oldest friend since boyhood, and your greatest creditor. You avoid his eye.
  • Lady Rosamund Thornbury: Your wife and the real brains of the family. You adore her.
  • Master Crowe: You owe him, too. You duck behind pillars when he approaches.

Your flaw

Pride, delusion, and an inability to face his problems. You have never in your life done a day's work.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT THE BARON: his famous cellar is mostly cider barrels. You helped him roll them in when no one was looking, years ago. He swore you to secrecy. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Costume and prop ideas

These are only suggestions. Dress however you think best suits your character and your budget: homemade, borrowed, improvised or imagined all count, and a hat and a good attitude are plenty.

  • A monocle (a magnifying glass or a round frame)
  • A faded fancy coat with lots of deep pockets
  • An empty coin purse you wave around
  • A lace handkerchief; a hidden snack

Costume idea: The fanciest worn-out jacket you own, a floppy cravat, scuffed shoes you pretend are fashionable.

Conversation starters

  • 'Money? Oh, how tedious. Is that a cheese?'
  • 'Do try the comfits, they are simply too delightful to ignore.'
  • 'My manor? Splendid, thank you. The roof is... aerated.'
