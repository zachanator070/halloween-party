Sir Cedric Aelfric Brightblade

Knight of the Realm, Champion of Queen Margarethe
Phase 1
Your Character

Prepared for Jared Beckstead. Keep this page secret!

Who you are

You are a famously brave knight, according to you, and you have come partly to hunt wolves in the Baron's woods tomorrow. You have fought in 'forty victories' (the number changes every time you say it) and you will tell anyone who will hold still. You are secretly terrified of ghosts, geese, and loud noises in the dark. It is All Hallows' Eve and you are NOT comfortable.

Your quirks (lean into them!)

Every story starts 'In my forty victories...' and the number changes each time ('thirty-eight', 'fifty-one'). You make tiny involuntary flinches at spooky noises (creaks, bells, giggles) and then say 'Hah! A knight is never startled!' very loudly.

How you treat others

  • Puff up your chest around other men of rank; challenge the Jester to a verbal duel and lose.
  • Bow low to the Queens and compliment their courage.
  • Treat your squire Wat with gruff affection and constantly ask him to hold something.
  • Avoid anything spooky; change the subject whenever ghosts, bats, or the dark are mentioned.

Your relationships

  • Squire Wat Fenwick: Your squire, your shadow, your biggest fan. You teach him everything (badly).
  • Queen Margarethe: You are her champion. You would die for her and she knows it and bosses you.
  • Baron Godfrey: He (and a merchant partner) lent you 50 marks, which you have not repaid. You avoid him by loudly telling stories.

Your flaw

A secret coward who is dying to be seen as a hero. You also owe money you pretend you don't.

Your secret to trade: ABOUT MAGISTER MORDECAI: his 'magical lightning' is flash powder. You saw him mixing it in the stable behind the royal tent last spring. Trade this for information.
You may reveal it in exchange for information or gold. Do not show anyone this page.

Gold coins and awards

You begin the feast with 10 gold coins (the host will hand them to you). Coins may be offered in trade for information: a secret, an answer, or a piece of evidence. Haggle as your character would. Turning in the evidence you are sent to find earns 2 more coins from the host.

At the end of the feast, prizes are given for: Richest Purse (most coins), Master Detective (everyone who names the murderer), and by vote of all guests, Best Costume and Best Actor.

Costume and prop ideas

These are only suggestions. Dress however you think best suits your character and your budget: homemade, borrowed, improvised or imagined all count, and a hat and a good attitude are plenty.

  • A cardboard, foam, or wooden sword with a scabbard
  • A shield (a lid, tray, or cardboard)
  • A tin-foil medal pinned to your chest
  • A colander, pot, or helmet-shaped hat

Costume idea: A tunic with a tabard over a long-sleeved shirt, a belt with a sword, boots, an enormous mustache (drawn or glued).

Conversation starters

  • 'In my forty victories, I have never seen such a feast!'
  • 'Ahem, who rang that bell? I was not startled.'
  • 'Wat! Hold this. And that. And my dignity.'
Keep this page secret. More will be revealed at the feast.
